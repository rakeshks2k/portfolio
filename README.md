## About Me

I'm **Rakesh K S**, an **Associate Product Manager based in Bengaluru, India**, with experience across **FinTech, B2B SaaS, and GenAI**.

At **Melento**, I've worked on **digital signature platforms, payment solutions, and AI-powered document tools**. My work spans **product discovery, customer research, product strategy, roadmap prioritization, analytics, and execution**, collaborating with engineering, design, and business teams to build solutions that simplify workflows and deliver business value.

I enjoy breaking down complex problems, identifying opportunities, and turning ideas into products that solve real customer needs.
