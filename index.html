About me

I've always been curious about how things work, and why they're sometimes made more complicated than they need to be. That curiosity is what drew me to product management: understanding people's problems and finding better ways to solve them.

Over the past 3+ years, I have worked across Fintech, B2B SaaS, and GenAI, building everything from digital signature and payment solutions to AI-powered document tools. At Melento, my work has contributed to products generating ₹29Cr+ in annual revenue and supporting 4.2Cr+ transactions.

What I enjoy most is taking an idea from “What if we did this better?” to something people actually use and businesses see value in. I like digging into problems, connecting the dots, and working with teams to turn good ideas into great products. To me, that’s what product management is all about.
